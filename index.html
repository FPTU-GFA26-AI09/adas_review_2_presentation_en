Establishing scientific boundaries and core technical contributions
Academic Gaps
🎯 Research Questions (RQ)
  • Main RQ: How to concurrently integrate Lane + Tracking + Metric Depth & TTC on a single RGB camera stream, achieving high accuracy while sustaining real-time throughput ≥ 30 FPS (tail latency p95 < 33 ms)?
  • Sub-RQ1 (Lane - Quan): How can Row-based Anchor Classification sustain F1 > 75% on CULane under low-light/shadows without expensive pixel segmentation compute?
  • Sub-RQ2 (Track - Triet): How does NMS-free Dual Label Assignment (YOLOv10) + ByteTrack quantitatively improve p95 Latency and ID persistence across BDD100K 10 classes?
  • Sub-RQ3 (Depth - Khang): Can Monocular Metric Depth (Depth Anything V2) achieve AbsRel < 0.12 to deliver early collision warnings with TTC ≥ 1.5s without LiDAR?
🔍 Academic Research Gaps
  • 1. Task Isolation Gap: Existing SOTA studies exclusively address isolated sub-problems (detection only, lane only, or depth only). End-to-end unified vision ADAS remains scarce.
  • 2. Latency Bottleneck Gap: Accurate Monocular Depth models are computationally heavy, making real-time coupling with multi-object tracking (≥ 30 FPS) highly challenging.
  • 3. Gradient Conflict Gap: Shared-backbone multitask architectures (e.g. YOLOPv2) suffer from severe gradient interference across task heads, degrading joint accuracy.

Slide 08 • Scientific Integrity

DATA LEAKAGE PREVENTION & KITTI SCOPE

Strict sequence-level isolation and full 834-frame unbroken temporal validation
Anti-Leakage Protocol
🔒 Strict Sequence-Level Partitioning
  • The Temporal Leakage Hazard: In video ADAS, randomly shuffling frames into train/val causes adjacent frames ($t$ and $t + 33\text{ ms}$) to appear in both sets, artificially inflating metrics through background memorization.
  • Sequence-Level Isolation: Training and evaluation sets are split strictly by independent video sequence IDs. Val sequences have 0% environmental overlap with training splits.
  • SHA-256 Checksum Verification: Every raw video frame, annotation file, and model checkpoint is verified via SHA256SUMS.txt to ensure complete bitwise reproducibility.

🔍 Four Scientific Discrepancy Drivers
  • 1. Domain Gap: Published papers benchmark on synthetic/curated laboratory splits. Real dashcam video suffers from sensor compression artifacts, low dynamic range, and lens distortion.
  • 2. Resolution Scaling: Downsampling ViT input to 518px reduces token quadratic overhead by 85%, introducing a minor +1.2% depth variance in exchange for a 4x latency speedup.
  • 3. Hardware Architectural Shift: Official benchmarks utilize enterprise server GPUs (RTX 3090 / A100) with 384-bit memory buses; Tesla T4 features a constrained 128-bit GDDR6 interface.
  • 4. Evaluation Protocol Rigor: The team enforces unbroken temporal sequence testing rather than cherry-picked random frame evaluations.

FULL-SYSTEM ERROR PROPAGATION MODELING

Quantifying error dampening from bounding box jitter through depth median to safety TTC
Cascade Modeling
⛓️ Cascaded Uncertainty Chain & Dampening
STAGE 1: 2D DETECTOR BOUNDING BOX JITTER
Pixel Boundary Noise: σ_bbox ± 2.1 px • Mitigated via ByteTrack Kalman smooth
STAGE 2: MONOCULAR DEPTH EXTRACTION
Depth Variance: σ_Z ± 0.35m (at 30m) • Dampened via 20% Adaptive ROI Inset (torch.median)
STAGE 3: APPROACH VELOCITY ESTIMATION
Velocity Error: σ_v ± 0.21 m/s • Dampened via Theil-Sen Robust Median Regression
STAGE 4: DOWNSTREAM SAFETY TIME-TO-COLLISION (TTC)
Final Decision Bound: ΔTTC ≤ 0.05 s • Stable alarm generation without panic braking
🔬 Error Dampening Proof
  • The Amplification Threat: In naive pipelines, numerical differentiation $\frac{\Delta Z}{\Delta t}$ exponentially magnifies pixel noise into velocity errors of $\pm 4.0\text{ m/s}$.
  • Two-Tier Non-Linear Dampening:
    • Spatial Dampening: 20% ROI Inset discards boundary asphalt pixels, bounding depth variance to $\pm 2.35\%$.
    • Kinematic Dampening: Theil-Sen evaluates all pairwise slopes $\text{median}\left(\frac{Z_j - Z_i}{t_j - t_i}\right)$, providing high robustness against transient outliers.
  • Result: Final TTC decision error remains tightly bounded at $\le 0.05\text{ seconds}$.
